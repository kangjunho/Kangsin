How to Use This Hub

INTERPRETATION PRINCIPLES

Questions to Ask Before Accepting a Result

Was the design appropriate?독립 표본, 대조군, pairing과 confounder가 적절한가?
Does the method match the data?자료의 분포, 구조와 연구질문에 맞는 model인가?
Is the effect meaningful?P-value뿐 아니라 effect size와 uncertainty가 충분한가?
Was leakage prevented?Feature selection, tuning과 validation이 독립적으로 수행됐는가?
Is biology validated?Database association을 mechanism이나 causality로 과장하지 않았는가?
Can it be reproduced?Data, code, version, parameter와 environment가 기록됐는가?
