Spatial transcriptomics of regional response heterogeneity in rectal cancer after neoadjuvant treatment

This study investigates regional heterogeneity in treatment response within a single rectal cancer specimen using CosMx spatial transcriptomics, focusing on cellular composition, spatial organization, functional alterations, and cell–cell communication in the tumor microenvironment.
